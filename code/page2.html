Harmony in Reflection

In Japan, there’s a cultural belief that ovals are symbolic of harmony and balance, which is why mirrors are often oval-shaped in traditional settings. The idea is that an oval mirror reflects the viewer in a way that feels softer and more harmonious compared to other shapes. It's said that oval mirrors offer a gentle way to "see oneself," aligning with Japan’s focus on balance and subtle beauty in design. This belief also ties into how reflection and self-awareness are viewed in Japanese culture—where one’s image is considered an ongoing reflection of inner peace and grace.
